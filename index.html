The Teacher Who Loves All Living Things 🌿

Hi! I'm Manu. At 26, I spend my days doing what I genuinely cherish: teaching Science and Biology to young students at Saint Mary's School, showing them that nature and science are the most magical real-life adventures on Earth!

With a Bachelor’s in Zoology, Botany & Chemistry, an M.Sc in Zoology from Guru Nanak Dev University, and ongoing B.Ed at Punjabi University, my mission is to inspire students to look through the microscope and fall in love with the wonders of biology.
